The fitting

Three steps to a fit.

No rig, no depth camera, no app install. A laptop webcam and a product image is the whole setup.

STEP 01 📷
Point the camera

Stand in frame. Body tracking locks onto your shoulders, torso and hands every frame, so the garment follows you instead of floating.

STEP 02 👕
Drop in the garment

Upload a t-shirt, shirt or jacket. Add a fabric pattern on top if you want to preview the same cut in a different material.

STEP 03 ✨
Watch it land

The model paints the garment onto your torso only. Your face, your room and everything outside the mask stay exactly as the camera saw them.

Under the hood

Built to run live.

Generator
SD 1.5 Inpainting
Garment painted into a torso mask, with IP-Adapter holding the reference.
Sampler
4-step LCM
Latent consistency scheduling, tuned for interactive frame rates.
Body tracking
MediaPipe Tasks
Selfie segmentation plus hand landmarks, so hands never get painted over.
Transport
WebSocket
Frame-drop gated — a stale frame is discarded rather than queued.
Preserved
Face & background
