The fitting

Three steps to a fit.

No rig, no depth camera, no app to install. A webcam and one product image is the whole setup.

Step one
Step into frame

Body tracking locks onto the shoulder line, torso and hands each frame, so the garment follows the customer instead of floating over them.

Step two
Hang the garment

Upload a t-shirt, shirt or jacket. Layer a fabric over it to preview the same cut in another material without a second sample.

Step three
Watch it settle

The garment is painted onto the torso only. The face, the room and everything beyond the mask stay exactly as the camera saw them.

Seven ways to work the mirror.
